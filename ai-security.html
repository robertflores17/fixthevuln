Securing AI & LLM Systems

Large language models and AI agents introduce a security surface that doesn't map cleanly onto traditional web application risks: prompt injection instead of SQL injection, model poisoning instead of supply chain tampering, excessive agency instead of privilege escalation. This hub collects everything on FixTheVuln for defending against it — the OWASP LLM Top 10 broken down technique by technique, deep dives on specific attack classes, career guidance for the emerging AI security field, and ongoing research coverage.
