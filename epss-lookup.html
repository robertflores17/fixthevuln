Bulk EPSS Score Lookup

Paste any number of CVE IDs and see which ones are most likely to be exploited

What is EPSS? The Exploit Prediction Scoring System, published daily by FIRST, estimates the probability that a CVE will see exploitation activity in the next 30 days. CVSS rates how bad a flaw is. EPSS estimates how likely attackers are to use it.

How to read the results

EPSS probability is the model's estimate, from 0% to 100%, that the CVE gets exploited in the next 30 days.

Percentile is where the CVE ranks against every other scored CVE. A 95th-percentile CVE scores higher than 95% of all CVEs.

Use EPSS alongside CVSS and the CISA KEV catalog. A CVE already in KEV is being exploited, whatever its EPSS score says. A CVE with no score is either too new or not yet in FIRST's data.

Data from the FIRST EPSS API. Lookups run in your browser. Your CVE list goes to FIRST only, never to FixTheVuln.
